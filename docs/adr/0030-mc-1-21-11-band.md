# ADR-0030: MC 1.21.11 gets its own band — the NeoForge SPI broke at FML 10.0

**Status:** Accepted (scaffold landed; compiles under `javac` against the real upstream jars, not
yet built under Gradle or boot-verified).

## Context

[ADR-0023](0023-multi-mc-band-publication.md) defines `mcdp-1.21` as covering **1.21.1 only**. The
1.21 line actually ran to **1.21.11** (2025-12-09, the last release before Mojang's switch to
calendar versioning), so ten releases — 1.21.2 … 1.21.11 — sit outside every band.

The cheap answer would be to *widen* `mcdp-1.21` to 1.21.x: one artifact, unchanged source, a
loosened `fabric.mod.json` floor. This repo has spent three rounds deleting duplicated band source
(ADR-0029 merged `forge-1.17` into the 4.0 adapter; every `fabric-*` band already shares a tree),
so a new subproject has to justify itself. The question is therefore narrow and empirical: **did
the surfaces mcdp actually calls move between 1.21.1 and 1.21.11?**

## Evidence

Read off upstream artifacts with `curl` + `javap`, then checked by compiling the trees with plain
`javac`. No Gradle build was run.

### Version pairing

| MC | NeoForge | `net.neoforged.fancymodloader:loader` | Fabric loader (fabric-meta) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.21.1 | 21.1.251 | **4.0.44** (catalog pins 4.0.42) | 0.16.x (catalog pins 0.16.9) |
| 1.21.4 | 21.4.157 | 6.0.18 | — |
| 1.21.8 | 21.8.54 | 9.0.18 | — |
| 1.21.10 | 21.10.64 | **10.0.32** | — |
| 1.21.11 | 21.11.45 | **10.0.36** | **0.19.5** |

`net.neoforged:bus` is **8.0.5** in both `neoforge-21.1.251.pom` and `neoforge-21.11.45.pom`, so
`BusBuilder` / `IEventBus` / `IModBusEvent` need no porting. `loader-10.0.36.pom` still depends on
`com.mojang:logging` and on `net.fabricmc:sponge-mixin:0.16.5+mixin.0.8.7` — Mixin is still 0.8.x,
so the bridge codegen's premise (ADR-0008/0018/0021) holds. `ModContainer.class` in loader 10.0.36
is class-file major **65**, so the band's `javaRelease` stays **21**.

### Fabric: unchanged, exactly as ADR-0023 asserts

`javap` on `fabric-loader-0.16.9.jar` vs `0.19.5.jar`: `LanguageAdapter`, `PreLaunchEntrypoint`,
`LanguageAdapterException` and `ModContainer` are identical, and `FabricLoader` is additive only
(0.19.5 adds `getRawGameVersion()`; nothing removed). ADR-0023's "stable across fabric-loader 0.14+"
claim is confirmed through **0.19.5**. The Fabric half of a 1.21.11 band is a `srcDirs` pointer and
a `depends` floor, nothing else.

### NeoForge: the core SPI is unchanged, but two surrounding surfaces broke

`javap` output is **byte-for-byte identical** between `loader-4.0.42.jar` and `loader-10.0.36.jar`
for `IModLanguageLoader` (still `loadMod(IModInfo, ModFileScanData, ModuleLayer)`), `IModInfo`,
`ModFileScanData` (including `getAnnotatedBy(Class, ElementType)`, the 9.0.x-era method the 1.20.6
band works around), `ModFileScanData$AnnotationData`, `IConfigurable`, `IModFile$Type`,
`AutomaticEventSubscriber.inject`, and `net.neoforged.fml.ModContainer` (same fields, same
`ModContainer(IModInfo)` ctor, same `protected constructMod()`, same `abstract getEventBus()`, and
**no `contextExtension`** in either — so ADR-0023's pre-21.x workaround stays inapplicable).

Two things the adapter uses are **gone** in FML 10.0.x:

1. **`IModFile.findResource(String...)` was removed.** FML 10 replaced the `SecureJar` view of a
   mod file with `net.neoforged.fml.jarcontents.JarContents` (`IModFile.getContents()`;
   `getSecureJar()` is gone too). `JarContents` is stream-addressed — `get`, `openFile`, `readFile`,
   `containsFile`, `findFile(String) -> Optional<URI>` — and exposes **no `java.nio.file.Path` for a
   jar entry**. `McdpLanguageLoader` calls `findResource` in three places (the `mcdepprovider.toml`
   read, the `mcdp-bridges.toml` read, and the `[[mixins]]` config walk).
2. **`FMLEnvironment.dist` stopped being a field.** 4.0.42 has `public static final Dist dist`;
   10.0.36 makes `FMLEnvironment` `final` with `static Dist getDist()`.
   `McdpModContainer.constructMod()` reads the field.

Both are **link-time** failures (`NoSuchMethodError`, `NoSuchFieldError`), not degradations — the
1.21.1 adapter jar cannot run on 1.21.11 at all. Bisecting: `loader-6.0.18` (MC 1.21.4) and
`loader-9.0.18` (MC 1.21.8) **both still have** `findResource(String...)`, `getSecureJar()` and the
`dist` field, so the break is precisely at the **FML 9.0 → 10.0 boundary, i.e. MC 1.21.10**.

Two further removals we do not use: `IModFileInfo.moduleName()` is gone, and `LoadingModList`
changed shape (`getMods()`, `getPlugins()`, `getModFileById` all survive). One soft signal, not a
break: `LoadingModList.get()` is `@Deprecated(forRemoval = true)` in FML 10 and 11, the replacement
being `FMLLoader.getCurrent().getLoadingModList()`. The adapter deliberately keeps `get()` — still
present in 11.0.15, and it is the form that returns `null` rather than throwing before FML init,
which is the contract the lazy-populator path depends on. Revisit when it is actually removed.

### Compile check (`javac`, no Gradle)

All three trees compiled with `javac --release 21` against the downloaded upstream jars
(`loader-10.0.36`, `mergetool-2.0.0-api` for `Dist`, `bus-8.0.5`, `com.mojang:logging`, slf4j, asm,
maven-artifact):

| Sources | Classpath | Result |
|---|---|---|
| `core/` + `deps-lib/` + **`fabric/`** | fabric-loader **0.16.9** | clean |
| `core/` + `deps-lib/` + **`fabric/`** | fabric-loader **0.19.5** | **clean** |
| `core/` + `deps-lib/` + **`neoforge/`** (canonical 21.x) | loader **10.0.36** | **5 errors** — the three `findResource` call sites, the `ensurePromotionInitialized` one, and `FMLEnvironment.dist` |
| `core/` + `deps-lib/` + **`neoforge-1.21.11/`** (this band) | loader **10.0.36** | clean (2 deprecation warnings, see above) |

Row 2 is the positive case for sharing the Fabric tree; row 3 is the negative case that forces the
NeoForge fork; row 4 is the port itself.

## Decision

**A separate `mcdp-1.21.11` band, with a forked NeoForge adapter and a shared Fabric adapter.**
Widening `mcdp-1.21` is rejected: it would publish one artifact whose NeoForge half link-errors on
half the versions the artifact claims. The `fabric.mod.json` floor is not the constraint; the FML
SPI is.

- `fabric-1.21.11/` — `setSrcDirs` at `fabric/src/main/{java,resources}`, same as every other
  Fabric band. `javaRelease = 21`, `fabricLoaderVersion = "0.19"`.
- `neoforge-1.21.11/` — **own source tree**, ported from `neoforge/`, compiled against
  `fancymodloader:loader:10.0.36`. `javaRelease = 21`, `fmlModType = LIBRARY` (the `LIBRARY` jar
  type and the `META-INF/services/…IModLanguageLoader` discovery path are unchanged in FML 10).
- `multi-1.21.11/` → publishes `de.lhns.mcdp:mcdp-1.21.11`.

**Band coverage.** The band is named `1.21.11` but the SPI evidence says it is really the
**FML 10.0 band**, i.e. it covers **MC 1.21.10 and 1.21.11**; the NeoForge test mod's range is
`[21.10,22)` / `[1.21.10,1.22)`. `mcdp-1.21` stays 1.21.1-only.

**1.21.2 … 1.21.9 remain uncovered**, deliberately. They span FML 5.0/6.0/7.0/8.0/9.0; each would
need its own compile pin and possibly its own port, for MC versions that are neither the line's
entry point (1.21.1, where the modded ecosystem sits) nor its tail. If demand appears, the 9.0 line
(1.21.5–1.21.8) is the cheapest add: it still has `findResource` and `FMLEnvironment.dist`, so the
canonical `neoforge/` source compiles against it unmodified — a pure `setSrcDirs` band.

### Two additive `core/` helpers

`JarContents` has no `Path`, but `McdpProvider.registerAutoBridgeManifestToml` and
`MixinConfigScanner.registerMixinOwnersFromConfigs` both took `Path`s even though both only ever
*read text* out of them. Rather than have the 1.21.11 adapter manufacture a synthetic `Path` (the
`jar:` URI `findFile` returns is not openable via `Path.of` unless a matching zip `FileSystem`
happens to be open), `core/` gained two **content-addressed** entry points:

- `McdpProvider.registerAutoBridgeManifestTomlContent(ModClassLoader, String toml, String source)`
- `MixinConfigScanner.registerMixinOwnersFromConfigContents(String modId, List<String> jsonContents)`

Both are purely additive. The `Path` overloads are unchanged in signature and behaviour;
`registerAutoBridgeManifestToml(ModClassLoader, Path)` now reads the file and delegates, so there is
one parser path. Every other band keeps calling the `Path` form.

## Consequences

**Positive.**

- The 1.21 line's tail is covered on both loaders, without disturbing `mcdp-1.21`.
- ADR-0023's Fabric-source-sharing claim is verified two major loader lines further out (0.19.5),
  which also de-risks the 26.x bands.
- `core/`'s bridge/mixin registration is no longer coupled to loader SPIs that expose `Path`s. Any
  future loader that goes stream-only (the direction of travel — NeoForge moved off `SecureJar`
  precisely to stop pretending jar entries are files) is now a smaller port.

**Negative.**

- A second NeoForge source tree, bringing the total to three (`neoforge/` for 21.x,
  `neoforge-1.20.6/` for 8.0.x, `neoforge-1.21.11/` for 10.0.x). The two 1.21-line trees differ by
  roughly twenty lines; they will drift.
- `mcdp-1.21`'s scope is now explicitly 1.21.1, with a ten-version hole between it and
  `mcdp-1.21.11`. That is a support-table wording problem as much as a code one.

**Unverified — flagged, not resolved.**

- **Nothing here was built under Gradle or booted.** `JarContents`' behaviour in a ModDevGradle
  *dev* run (content roots are source-set output directories, not a jar) has not been observed;
  `readManifest` keeps a `FileSystems.newFileSystem(modFile)` fallback for that reason.
- **The Fabric test mod's Loom pin.** The root wrapper is Gradle 8.11.1 and CI invokes test mods
  through `../../gradlew`; Loom 1.10+ needs Gradle 8.12, so the newest Loom available is the 1.9
  line, and whether 1.9 can provision MC 1.21.11 is unconfirmed. Remedies: a root wrapper bump plus
  a current Loom (1.18.2 is newest), or a committed per-mod wrapper as `forge-example-1.18` has.
  Until settled the 1.21.11 cells stay out of the CI smoke matrix.
- **MDG 2.0.147 against NeoForge 21.11.45** was chosen on the ADR-0023 pairing rule (MDG must
  post-date the NeoForge build, which it does by nine months) but not exercised.

## Postscript: this also breaks every 26.x NeoForge band

Recorded because it changes an ADR-0023 claim rather than just this band's scope.
`net.neoforged:neoforge:26.1.2.109` declares `fancymodloader:loader:11.0.15`, and `javap` on that
jar shows the same two removals as FML 10 (`IModFile.getContents()` and no `findResource`;
`FMLEnvironment.getDist()` and no `dist` field; `getAnnotatedBy` survives). `neoforge-26.1`
currently pins `loader:4.0.42` and shares `neoforge/`'s source on the premise that "the SPI has not
in fact diverged between 21.x and 26.1" (ADR-0023, "Source structure"). That premise has expired:
`mcdp-26.1`'s NeoForge half would link-error against any current 26.1 build.

`loader:12.0.0` (NeoForge 26.3) is the same: `IModFile.getContents()` and no `findResource`,
`FMLEnvironment.getDist()` and no `dist` field. Every member this band's port calls is present and
identically signed in 12.0.0, so the FML-10 port is the right adapter for the whole 10/11/12
generation, not just this band. The fix is mechanical: any 26.x NeoForge band points its `srcDirs` at
`neoforge-1.21.11/src/main/java` and compiles against `loader:10.0.36`. (Not 11.x/12.x: those jars
are class-file major 69, which javac on the root JDK-21 toolchain refuses to read — `javap` reads
them, which is how the above was established. 10.0.36 is major 65 and API-compatible for everything
the adapter calls.) Not done here: those are different bands, under concurrent work.
